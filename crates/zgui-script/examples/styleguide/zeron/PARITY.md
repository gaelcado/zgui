# Toward old Zeron's parity

What Zeron does (`comet/crates/ui/src`) and where this app stands, in any
of its layouts. The goal is feature and quality parity, drawn in the new
layouts. Data here is sample data until the engine is wired. ✓ done, ◐
started, ○ not yet.

The views are the kit's (`kit/session_sidebar`, `command_palette`,
`space_tabs`, `settings_page`, `well`, `session_card`, `titlebar`); the
modules here hold the app's data, state and composition, and the workbench
renders the same components.
