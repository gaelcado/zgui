## Variable-height list

<WinScene scene="vlist" />

> **Why zgx's fling shows long frames here.** The list's fling ends slowly, under a pixel a refresh for its last ticks (160 px a tick decaying to 0.6). zgx scrolls in whole device pixels, as Chrome and Windows' own lists do, to keep text crisp, so in that tail some refreshes move nothing on screen and the capture counts them as long frames, though zgx presented on every refresh, its GPU work done within 0.2 ms of each present. At a steady 12 or 3 px a tick it shows none. Rune draws at fractional positions, which change its pixels on every tick.
